# Three Arrows Capital — research dossier

**Research cutoff:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** Structured candidate; not yet approved for public release.

## Classification
Crypto investment fund collapse and cross-border liquidation

## Core reconstruction
Following the market downturn and Terra ecosystem collapse in May 2022, 3AC suffered substantial losses and lenders issued default notices. The BVI High Court placed the fund into liquidation on June 27, 2022 and appointed joint liquidators. The fund sought Chapter 15 protection in the United States on July 1, 2022. The FSOC report described the failure as a source of contagion across crypto markets, with counterparties including Voyager and Genesis suffering large exposures. In 2025, Singapore's Court of Appeal upheld important disclosure orders sought by liquidators against the fund's directors, while varying the orders concerning examination of one director.

## Causal analysis
Analysis: 3AC's failure illustrates how leverage and concentrated counterparty exposure can turn a market downturn into a network-wide credit event. Losses associated with Terra and the broader crypto sell-off weakened the fund while lenders and counterparties had significant claims against it. The sources reviewed establish liquidation, cross-border proceedings and large reported creditor exposures, but do not provide a complete independently verified portfolio-level attribution of every loss. The case should therefore avoid presenting one trade or one token as the sole cause.

## Current status and limits
3AC entered BVI liquidation in June 2022, followed by Chapter 15 proceedings in the United States and recognition of the foreign liquidation in Singapore. Government analysis described the fund's collapse as a source of crypto-market contagion. Court-document reporting identified approximately $3.5 billion owed to 27 crypto firms, but this is a reported creditor-claim figure rather than a final recovery amount. Singapore court proceedings continued into 2025, including appellate litigation over directors' disclosure and examination. The current total creditor recovery and final liquidation outcome were not established by the sources reviewed for this batch and are left open for further verification.

## Warning-sign discipline
Warning signs distinguish what was reasonably observable before the incident, what became visible during it, and what only became clear through later proceedings. Do not relabel hindsight evidence as a pre-incident warning.
