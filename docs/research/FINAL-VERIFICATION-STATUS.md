# CRYPTO GRAVEYARD — CORRECTED EVIDENCE & VERIFICATION STATUS

**Evidence cutoff:** 19 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Consolidated replacement for the prior final verification pack, incorporating the newly supplied primary bankruptcy filings for FTX, Terraform Labs, and Celsius Network.

## The short answer: is the information verified?

**Some of it is verified strongly enough to write the core case narratives. Not every detail across all eight cases is fully verified, and not every case is ready for publication.**

Verification is claim-by-claim, not a single yes/no label for an entire case. A primary court judgment can establish a conviction or finding; it does not automatically verify every technical explanation, recovery figure, allegation, or current administrative status in the same case.

The newly inspected filings materially improve three cases:
- FTX: D.I. 36748, the official post-confirmation report for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
- Terraform: D.I. 1392, the Plan Administrator's Fourth Status Update filed November 13, 2026.
- Celsius: D.I. 8488, the Plan Administrator's Ninth Status Report on Distributions filed August 31, 2026.

They are sufficient to support the particular facts stated below. They do not remove every remaining verification task.

### 4. Celsius — D.I. 8488

**Document:** Plan Administrator's Ninth Status Report on Distributions.  
**Filed:** August 31, 2026.  
**Reporting period:** May 1 through July 31, 2026.  
**Case:** In re Celsius Network LLC, et al., No. 22-10964 (MEW), Bankr. S.D.N.Y.

Verified figures:
- Approximately **$3.51 billion** eligible for distribution.
- Approximately **$3.47 billion**, or **99.03%**, successfully distributed as of July 31, 2026.
- Fourth distribution: **$318 million** eligible; **$303 million** distributed; approximately **73,500** creditors received it.
- Exhibit A, page 18 reports **$300 million in net assets**: $15 million crypto, $79 million fiat, and $206 million Litigation Administrator assets.
- The same exhibit separately reports **$278 million remaining amount owed in current distributions**: $262 million crypto and $16 million fiat.

**Critical correction:** $300 million net assets and $278 million remaining amount owed in current distributions are not the same measure. The $206 million Litigation Administrator assets are not simply cash ready to distribute. Use the report's own definitions.

**Interpretation:** The filing provides a strong distribution-status snapshot through July 31, 2026. It is not a current November 2026 distribution report. A later report or docket update is still needed before stating the latest completion status.

Official distribution portal: https://celsiusdistribution.stretto.com/

### Terra / LUNA — CONDITIONAL APPROVAL
The SEC jury verdict/settlement and Do Kwon criminal sentence are supported by official records. D.I. 1392 now supplies current November 2026 claims-process evidence. It does not establish that ordinary crypto-loss distributions have started. A stronger independent technical reconstruction of the UST/LUNA mechanism and a later trust-status update would improve the technical and recovery sections.

### BitConnect — CONDITIONAL APPROVAL
The founder indictment is an accusation, not a conviction. Promoter proceedings and Arcaro's sentence are separately documented. Keep individual legal outcomes separate. Contemporaneous platform shutdown evidence and the founder's current docket status remain useful final checks.

### OneCoin — CONDITIONAL APPROVAL
Greenwood's guilty plea and sentence, the absence of a genuine public/verifiable blockchain described in court-related records, and the FBI's June 2026 wanted status are supported. The remission process had a June 30, 2026 application deadline; a post-deadline administrator update would improve the current recovery section. Do not imply victims are guaranteed compensation.

### Three Arrows Capital — CONDITIONAL APPROVAL
The bankruptcy/liquidation background and Singapore Court of Appeal decision have primary support. The reported 35.16% recovery estimate attributed to the March 24, 2026 liquidators' report has not been directly verified against that primary report in this pack. Do not present it as a confirmed final recovery rate.

### Celsius Network — CONDITIONAL APPROVAL
Mashinsky's guilty plea and sentence are supported by DOJ records. D.I. 8488 now verifies the distribution snapshot through July 31, 2026 and corrects the distinction between net assets and amounts still owed in current distributions. Obtain a later distribution report before presenting this as the latest status.

### Thodex — HOLD
The January 2025 appellate reversal/remand is supported by reporting, but the full authenticated Turkish appellate judgment and the post-remand November 2026 outcome have not been verified in this pack. The prosecutor's final opinion is not a judgment. Do not publish a definitive retrial outcome or sentencing result without a court record or authoritative confirmation.
