# BitConnect — research dossier

**Research cutoff:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** Structured candidate; not yet approved for public release.

## Classification
Ponzi-style investment fraud; founder indicted and promoter guilty pleas and judgments

## Core reconstruction
BitConnect shut down its Lending Program in January 2018 after regulatory pressure and cease-and-desist actions; BCC's price fell sharply. The DOJ's 2022 indictment alleged that the founder and co-conspirators operated the program as a Ponzi scheme and manipulated BCC's price after the lending program closed. Glenn Arcaro pleaded guilty and received a 38-month sentence in September 2022. DOJ announced a victim distribution exceeding $17 million in January 2023. In September 2026, the SEC reported final judgments against three additional promoters. Kumbhani's indictment remains an accusation; DOJ's available update described him as a fugitive.

## Causal analysis
Analysis: the model depended on opaque trading claims, unusually high promised returns and a referral-driven promotional structure. Investors could not independently verify the claimed trading-bot performance or source of returns. Prosecutors alleged that incoming investor funds were recycled to pay earlier participants. The closure removed the central advertised product and was followed by a collapse in BCC's market price. The strongest legal conclusions apply to individuals who pleaded guilty or were subject to judgments; the founder's indictment is not a conviction.

## Current status and limits
Glenn Arcaro pleaded guilty and was sentenced to 38 months in September 2022. Founder Satish Kumbhani was indicted in February 2022; DOJ later described him as a fugitive, and this file does not treat the indictment as a conviction. In January 2023, DOJ announced more than $17 million in forfeited assets for victim compensation. In September 2026, the SEC reported final judgments against three promoters in a separate civil enforcement case. These outcomes do not establish identical liability for every promoter or affiliate.

## Warning-sign discipline
Warning signs distinguish what was reasonably observable before the incident, what became visible during it, and what only became clear through later proceedings. Do not relabel hindsight evidence as a pre-incident warning.
