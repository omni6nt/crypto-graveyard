# Thodex — research dossier

**Research cutoff:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** Structured candidate; not yet approved for public release.

## Classification
Centralized exchange collapse and criminal proceedings; latest retrial outcome not independently verified

## Core reconstruction
In April 2021, Thodex's website became inaccessible and users reported that withdrawals were unavailable. Turkish prosecutors opened an investigation and MASAK blocked the company's bank accounts. Founder Faruk Fatih Özer was arrested in Albania in 2022 and extradited to Turkey in April 2023. In January 2025, Anadolu Agency reported that an Istanbul regional appellate court reversed parts of the trial judgment: the organized-crime count was set aside and the case returned to the trial court, while detention relating to fraud and money-laundering allegations continued. This research has not independently verified a later final retrial judgment as of November 19, 2026.

## Causal analysis
Analysis: the case demonstrates the risk of concentrated custody at a centralized exchange and the importance of withdrawal functionality, operational transparency and reliable access to customer assets. The available sources establish the user-facing shutdown and the subsequent investigation, but they do not provide a sufficiently complete primary forensic record to state exactly how all customer assets were handled, the final verified number of victims, or the definitive total loss. The 2025 appellate remand also means the original trial outcome cannot simply be described as the final legal position.

## Current status and limits
Özer was arrested in Albania in August 2022 and extradited to Turkey in April 2023. A Turkish trial court later imposed a very long sentence, but in January 2025 the Istanbul regional appellate court reversed parts of that judgment and returned the case to the trial court. Anadolu Agency reported that Özer's release on the organized-crime count did not end his detention on fraud and money-laundering allegations. The current final outcome of the remanded proceedings has not been independently established in this research batch. No definitive verified total of customer losses or completed repayment is stated.

## Warning-sign discipline
Warning signs distinguish what was reasonably observable before the incident, what became visible during it, and what only became clear through later proceedings. Do not relabel hindsight evidence as a pre-incident warning.
