INSTALL
-------
1. Stop the local server with Ctrl+C.
2. Back up these files:
   index.html
   src/js/main.js
3. Extract this ZIP into C:\dev\crypto-graveyard and allow replacement.
4. Start the server:
   cd C:\dev\crypto-graveyard
   npx http-server . -p 8000
5. Hard refresh the browser with Ctrl+Shift+R.

WHAT TO TEST
------------
- Open the Cemetery entrance scene.
- Click "Begin the Visit".
- The title and captions should change sequentially, with a fade between captions.
- The gate lock should glow and release.
- The two wrought-iron leaves should swing open in opposite directions.
- The background should slowly push forward.
- The Coroner illustration should rise from the grave.
- Only one dialogue panel should be visible during the Coroner's welcome.
- "Enter the grounds" should switch to the grounds scene.
- "Skip cinematic" should jump to the final Coroner welcome.
- Sound is OFF by default. The Sound button enables simple synthesized atmospheric cues.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------
- The gate panels are bespoke SVG artwork animated in 3D CSS transforms. They are not a pre-rendered film.
- The Coroner is a newly generated illustrated pose with a rise animation. It is not yet a frame-by-frame skeletal character rig; facial acting, independent arm/lantern movement, and multiple body poses are still future animation work.
- The sound cues are synthesized browser tones, not a finished recorded sound design.
- The existing cemetery environment background is reused. This batch does not replace the full cemetery environment.
- The ground disturbance is a layered effect; detailed soil fracture simulation is a later polish task.
- Test desktop and mobile. The mobile layout adapts the Coroner/dialogue composition.

Do not commit or push until the owner reviews the experience.
